My name is Owais Qayoom, and I'm from Pampore, Kashmir — a town known for its saffron fields, and the place that shapes how I see craft, patience and detail in everything I build.

I completed my 12th grade from Boys Higher Secondary School Samboora, and I'm currently pursuing my studies at GIT + AI, where I'm developing my skills across artificial intelligence, Git/GitHub, programming, web technologies and motion graphics.

I'm a young, curious learner — drawn equally to the logic of code and the rhythm of motion design. My goal is to grow into a creative technologist who can move fluidly between building intelligent systems and designing the visuals that bring them to life.
